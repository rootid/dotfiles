# Global preferences

Applies to every project. A repo's own CLAUDE.md takes precedence.

## Before acting
- Stop and ask before anything irreversible or public: force-push, `git reset --hard`
  or anything else that discards uncommitted work, deleting files, pushing, opening
  or merging PRs, posting to external services.
- Look at uncommitted changes before touching them; they may be mine.

## How to work
- Follow the repo's existing conventions (naming, structure, comment density) over
  general best practice.
- Verify changes by running something real (syntax check, tests, a dry run), not by
  re-reading the code.

## How to report
- Keep replies short. Lead with the result.
- Show the commands that were run and whether they worked. Say plainly when
  something failed or was skipped.
